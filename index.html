01 / THE CHALLENGE

An answer is only the beginning of case preparation.

A legal research answer can be useful, yet still disconnected from the documents, people, events and questions that determine the next step. Locke Research Companion connects each stage in one case file.

Ask an issue, restrict the jurisdiction and court scope, inspect retrieved sources, upload case documents, review the chronology and facts, and prepare a documented attorney conversation.

This is an application architecture story—not a claim that an AI model replaces legal research judgment.

04 / ASK LOCKE

Clear research modes.
Visible trust boundaries.

Quick mode streams useful orientation. Source-Backed and Deep modes retrieve legal sources and **withhold the answer if the current citation audit blocks it**. Users can examine citations and keep the work connected to their case history.

Matching an answer's references to retrieved source metadata is not the same as checking legal propositions or negative treatment. The system's published evidence explains that distinction.
