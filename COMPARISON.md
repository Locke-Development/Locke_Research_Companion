# WHY CHOOSE LOCKE RESEARCH COMPANION?
### Legal research connected to an actual case record — a product engineering portfolio

**November 13, 2026 · Audience: future software-development clients and employers, not legal-service shoppers.**

> **Portfolio thesis:** Locke Research Companion demonstrates the integration of jurisdiction-aware research, source-matched AI answers, conversational continuity, private documents, OCR, timelines, facts, evidence, attorney preparation, reports and export into **one matter-centered application**. The differentiation is a connected product with visible trust boundaries—not an unsupported claim that it outperforms every major legal database.

## What Locke actually demonstrates

**1. Research in context.** Jurisdiction and court scope belong to the case, so answers, documents, facts, dates and later work share context.

**2. Verified source matching before display.** The display gate blocks Source-Backed and Deep output when the cited items cannot be matched to the retrieved sources. **This is not full quotation/proposition/negative-history verification** and is presented accurately as such.

**3. Documents become structured work.** Uploads can be extracted or OCR-reviewed, then used for chronology, issue tracking, fact validation, attorney preparation and multi-document analysis.

**4. Product-level engineering.** JWT/refresh sessions, tenant isolation, durable OCR jobs, token accounting, failover, cited document linking, PDF/DOCX exports and administration form a substantial end-to-end product implementation.

## Competitive advantages Locke should not claim

- Lexis+ with Protégé has Shepard's status/treatment signals, exclusive secondary law, Vaults, drafting and greater breadth.
- CoCounsel Legal uses Westlaw/Practical Law, KeyCite and Deep Research Verify, advanced litigation analyzer and extensive document processing.
- vLex Vincent has international legal-content breadth, argument building, 50-state surveys and richer judicial proceeding/document workflows.
- Bloomberg Law includes editorial guidance, dockets, legal news, litigation analytics and BCITE.
- Paxton supports case-law research, AI citations, drafted motions, file analysis and an AI Citator.

Locke's own source reports incomplete items: deeper citation verification, directly ingested state statutes, in-drawer attachment/jurisdiction controls, some chat actions, and currently unset commercial pricing.
